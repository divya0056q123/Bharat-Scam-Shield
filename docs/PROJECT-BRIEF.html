Investor awareness for India

Pause before you trust a financial message.

Pause. Check. Understand.

Bharat Scan Shield helps retail investors inspect suspicious financial content, understand its warning signs, and choose safer next steps.

The problem

Investment pitches arrive through WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, and social media. Guaranteed returns, urgency, fake authority claims, and payment requests can pressure people to act before they verify.

How it works

01 · SubmitPaste text, speak, or select a screenshot.
02 · InspectSee matched phrases and weighted warning signals.
03 · VerifyReview practical next steps and check official sources.

Signals it checks

Guaranteed or unusually high returns, urgency, requests for money or sensitive details, investment groups, authority claims, crypto/app pitches, and suspicious links.

Designed for real use

  • English and Hindi interface and explanations.
  • Text, voice input, and browser-side screenshot OCR.
  • Matched phrases make warnings explainable, not just a score.

Trust and transparency

  • Scanned text is sent to the analysis service but not stored by the app.
  • Screenshot files stay on the user's device; OCR runs in the browser.
  • Optional AI sends text to the configured provider; Gemini free-tier content may be used to improve Google's products.
  • Recent checks are kept only in the current browser tab.
